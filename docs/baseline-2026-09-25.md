## Finding: fuel trim is high only at idle

Warm engine (coolant ≥ 80 °C), closed loop (commanded λ 0.98–1.02), medians per band. Positive means the ECU is adding fuel.

| RPM band | Time in band | Short-term | Long-term | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Idle, < 1,000 | 110 s | +3.9% | +4.7% | **+8.6%** |
| 1,000–1,800 | 260 s | −1.6% | +3.1% | +0.8% |
| 1,800–2,500 | 389 s | 0.0% | +0.8% | +0.8% |
| > 2,500 | 144 s | −1.6% | +1.6% | 0.0% |

A fixed leak is a large share of the little air the engine pulls at idle and a negligible share at cruise, so a correction that shows up only at idle points to unmetered air getting in past the sensors. On the EA888 Gen 3 the usual culprit is a torn PCV valve diaphragm. +8.6% is within the ECU's normal range and won't set a code: worth tracking, not urgent.

Next: replace the PCV valve, log with the tag `post-pcv`, and compare the idle row.

## Boost, fueling and timing

| Condition | Rows | RPM | Load | Boost | λ actual / cmd | Timing | Rail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warm idle | 623 | 799 | 25% | 0 psi | 0.997 / 1.00 | −2.5° | 57 bar |
| Steady cruise | 1,783 | 1,932 | 20% | 0.9 psi | 0.998 / 1.00 | +16.5° | 132 bar |
| Under boost (> 3 psi) | 367 | 1,963 | 32% | 3.3 psi | 0.997 / 1.00 | +12.0° | 167 bar |
| Peak boost | 1 | 1,987 | 73% | 9.7 psi | 1.005 / 0.999 | −3.5° | 198 bar |

- Fueling stayed on target under load, and rail pressure reached 203 bar.
- Timing dropped to −3.5° at the boost peak. Low timing at high load and low rpm is normal on this engine, but standard OBD can't separate planned timing from knock retard. Per-cylinder knock values over UDS will.
- This ECU doesn't report commanded boost, so boost-target tracking can't be measured over OBD.
- There's no full-throttle data yet. A few 3rd-gear pulls from ~2,000 rpm belong in the baseline before any tune.

## Data quality

- Sampling had no gaps longer than 0.17 s.
- The drive ended with a power cut (the car's USB port), so its metadata has no row count. That's what prompted the 30 s metadata refresh and the `complete` flag.
- The wall clock jumped forward once the Pi reached NTP through the phone hotspot, which is why logs now carry a `clock_ok` column. `elapsed_s` is unaffected.
- `boost_cmd_kpa` and `o2s2_trim_pct` are always blank: this ECU doesn't report them.
